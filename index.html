Circle of fifths

Click a key. Outer ring = major, inner = relative minor. Neighbours share 6 of 7 notes — that's why moving one step around the circle sounds smooth, and why IV and V are the "closest" chords to I.

Key of C major

CAGED system

Any major chord can be played in 5 shapes that tile the whole neck: C, A, G, E, D — in that order going up. Each shape connects to the next (they share notes at the boundary). Learn the shapes ⇒ you know where the chord tones are everywhere ⇒ your solos land on chord tones. The pentatonic/major scale "boxes" sit on top of each shape.

Modes of the major scale

Same 7 notes, different home base. Start the C major scale on D and treat D as home — that's D Dorian. The "flavor" comes from where the half-steps fall relative to the new root. Click a row to put it on the fretboard.
